Frequently asked

  • My progress disappeared. Sign in with the same email (or Google/Apple) you used before — your save is stored in the cloud per account. If it still looks wrong, email us with your account email.
  • How do I delete my account? In-game: Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently removes your account and all data. You can also request deletion by email.
  • Restore purchases. Office → Store → Restore Purchases (works on the iOS app with the same Apple ID).
  • Playing as guest. You can play without an account; progress stays on your device. Create an account any time to save it to the cloud.
