What data exists, and where

Facebook / Instant Games players

When you play BenelOil on Facebook, your game progress is stored by Meta using the Instant Games player-data service. We do not operate an account for you, we do not ask for your email address, and no personal data is transferred to our servers. We never receive your name, profile picture, or friend list.

To delete this data:

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find BenelOil and select Remove.
  3. Tick the option to delete the data associated with the game, or use Send Request.

Removing the game deletes the stored progress held for you. You may also email us using the button above and we will confirm the deletion in writing.

Website and iOS players (accounts with an email address)

If you created an account at petrol.benerits.com or in the iOS app, we store your email address and your saved game. You can delete both:

  • In the game: Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently deletes the account and the saved game immediately.
  • By email: write to oguz@hopsule.com from the address registered to the account.

What we delete

  • Your account record (email address and password hash)
  • Your saved game and all progress
  • Any support messages or feedback linked to your account

Aggregate, non-identifying statistics (for example the total number of players active on a given day) are not linked to any individual and are not affected.

Timing

We acknowledge every request within 72 hours and complete deletion within 30 days. If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain why in writing.
